# Play Store page: text to paste

**App name** (max 30): `quiKey`

**Short description** (max 80):
`Big tiles that type your whole messages for you. No letter keys.`

**Full description** (max 4000):

```
quiKey is a keyboard made of big, easy tiles. There are no letter keys.

Save the messages you send again and again. Mark the words that change each time:
a person from a list, a time, a date, a number, or "10 mins from now".
When you use a message, quiKey asks one simple question at a time, with big buttons.
Then it types the whole message for you.

EXAMPLE
Message: "{child}, {medicine}, {dose}, {time} next dose not before {time+4h}"
You tap Sam, Calpol, 5ml, Now.
quiKey types: "Sam, Calpol, 5ml, 20:20 next dose not before 00:20"

GOOD FOR
- Medicine and care logs
- "Running late" and "I will be there in 10 mins" messages
- Sick-day and absence messages
- Any message you send the same way again and again

FEATURES
- Big tiles and folders, easy to tap
- Three looks (Calm, Bold, Squares), light and dark
- Quick choices for times and dates that you pick yourself
- Times worked out for you ("next dose 4 hours later")
- Large text option
- Back up and restore your quiKies
- Example quiKies to start with, and a short tour

PRIVATE
quiKey has no internet access. What you type stays on your phone.
```
