# quiKey privacy policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

quiKey does **not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.**

- quiKey has **no internet permission**. It cannot send anything off your phone.
- Your quiKies (saved messages, folders and answers) are stored **only on your phone**.
- The keyboard types the message you choose. It does **not** read or record what you type in other apps.
- There are **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.
- If you use Android's built-in backup (Google), Android may keep a copy of your quiKies in your own Google account. That is handled by Android, not by quiKey.
- The "Back up" feature in Settings saves a file that **you** choose where to keep.

If you delete the app, the data on your phone is deleted with it.
