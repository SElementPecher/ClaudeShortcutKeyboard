quiKey 1.1

On your phone, tap quiKey.apk below to download it, then open it to install. It installs over the version you have and keeps your quiKies.

What's new
- The quiKey logo on the keyboard, in the app, in Settings and in the tour.
- Mix and match: the keyboard can have a different look from the app (Settings > Preferences > Keyboard look).
- Swipe left and right on the keyboard to turn the page. Only the tiles move.
- Select several quiKies, then Move them to any folder or Home, or Export just those to a file.
- Send after adding (Settings > Preferences): the keyboard can press the app's Send button too, where the message box has one. For WhatsApp, Telegram and Signal it tells you which of their settings to turn on.
- Making a quiKey: every kind of changing part now opens its own screen; a number can be named; Enter adds a new line in a list.
- A friendlier tour: the changing word rolls, a hand-drawn arrow points at it, and you can swipe between pages.

Fixes
- Delete in select mode can no longer remove tiles that are not on screen.
- The editor keeps answers added from the keyboard while it was open, and Duplicate keeps unsaved edits.
- "+ New" from a chat app takes you back to the chat afterwards.
- Pressing Back on an unchanged quiKey no longer says "Saved".
- You always hear back after a backup.
- Smaller fixes, and a faster number pad and Exercise book screens.
